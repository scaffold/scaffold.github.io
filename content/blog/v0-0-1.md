Over 7 years ago I began thinking about a decentralized system built on proof of WASM computation instead of hash evaluations. Today, I'm launching v0.0.1. The version number slightly understates the amount of thinking, specifying, coding, discarding ideas, prompting, deleting code, simplification, back to human coding, architecting, and even occasionally dreaming of p2p networks, that has happened since the idea was conceived. I'm extremely proud of what Scaffold has become. It has a long way to go but it's a solid system that I believe has potential to become one of the foundations of the internet.

At its core Scaffold is a consensus protocol. It's a system of incentives that enables easily and quickly creating blocks (requests or responses) and aggregating it in a self-correcting, queryable way. Almost every operation in Scaffold runs in O(log N) resources (both space and time) wrt the total size of the block graph.
